Match the tiles. Restore the mansion.

Iznik Tiles is a relaxing yet challenging triple tile match puzzle inspired by the timeless art of Ottoman Iznik ceramics. Tap three tiles with the same motif to clear them, plan your moves across layered boards, and use your stars and tile shards to bring a ruined Ottoman mansion back to life, room by room.

  • 200 hand-tuned levels, every one guaranteed solvable
  • 18 hand-crafted Iznik motifs: tulips, carnations, pomegranates and more
  • New twists as you progress: pattern tiles, hidden tiles, locked tiles and a narrow tray
  • Helpful jokers: Undo, Hint, Shuffle and Release
  • Restore the courtyard, hall, hammam and garden of a historic mansion
  • No lives, no timers, no purchases — completely free
