Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026

This policy explains how Iznik Tiles: Triple Match (“the game”) handles information. The game is developed and published by Yunus Emre Söğüt, an independent developer based in Türkiye (“we”, “us”).

In short: the game has no accounts and never asks for your name, email or other personal details. Your progress is stored only on your device. The game is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob, which may collect device information as described below.

1. Information stored on your device

The game saves your level, stars, tile shards, jokers, restoration progress and settings (sound, music, vibration, language) in a file on your device. This data is not sent to us or to anyone else. It is deleted when you uninstall the game.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows banner, interstitial and optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • device identifiers, such as the advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, only if you allow tracking; Advertising ID on Android),
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • ad interactions (ads shown, taps) and diagnostic data.

Google uses this information to deliver ads, limit how often you see them, measure their performance, prevent fraud and, where you have given consent, show personalised ads. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Your choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: on first launch we ask for your consent using Google’s consent form. You can change your choice at any time in the game: Settings (gear icon) → Ad privacy settings.
  • iOS: the game asks for permission before using the advertising identifier. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads or Privacy → Ads).

If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalised.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not collect personal information ourselves and do not run our own servers for the game.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools other than Google AdMob.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • The game has no in-app purchases, chat or social features.

5. Children

The game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will help.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Türkiye’s Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698, “KVKK”), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Because we do not hold personal data about you, requests regarding advertising data are best directed to Google, but you are always welcome to contact us and we will assist you.

7. Data retention and transfers

We do not retain any personal data. Google retains advertising data according to its own policies and may process it on servers outside your country.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are published on this page with a new effective date.
